WHAT I’M WORKING TOWARD

Built from the problem outward.

Salesforce at the center

The goal is a document workflow rooted in Salesforce records, with Salesforce Files playing a part in the experience.

A better design workspace

A visual designer with room for your content, fields, and controls. Built to make working on a template feel manageable.

A practical migration path

Existing templates are the starting point. Complex layouts and formulas are being worked through as development continues.

A FEW FAIR QUESTIONS

Here’s where things stand.

Can I use the product today?

The product is still in development. Get in touch if you’d like to discuss your document needs and potential early access. A general release date hasn’t been announced.

Can I bring my existing templates?

That’s part of the plan. Import capabilities are being developed against real templates, with complex layouts and formulas getting particular attention. Not every template will transfer unchanged.

How much will it cost?

Pricing hasn’t been announced. This is being developed as a commercial product.

Can you help with my Salesforce work now?

Yes. Salesforce consulting and custom development are available independently of this project. Explore consulting services.
